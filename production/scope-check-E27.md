## Summary
E26 closed at I300 (G26.0 PASS; 837/837; build exit 0 x2 deterministic; dist 118 files / 1809.9 KB / headroom 238.1 KB; vendor sha256 13/13). E27 is the next art arc from the standing user directive (Blender art improvement), scoped from fresh live evidence at I301 (.evidence/I301/): the asteroid family - the most-seen geometry in the game (rock pool cap 24, concurrent 29; rocks are on screen 100% of play time) - still reads as a FIELD OF UNIFORM WARM-BROWN BLOBS across waves 1-3 despite the E18 warm-palette pass, the I264 per-facet value mottling, the I279 re-centered per-size Perlin field (per-rock base shift), and the E22 warm silhouette rim. Three measured causes, in order of leverage: (1) LOW VALUE SPREAD on the mid rocks - M's shadow facet p10 is 0.501 (nearly as bright as its lit p90 0.928) and M is the BRIGHTEST class (mean 0.721), so M reads flat/lump-like at on-screen size and the size-to-value ladder is arbitrary, not a natural depth order; (2) CONVEX-BLOB SILHOUETTE - all three size classes are smooth convex low-poly bodies with no crater/ridge break, so the mottling averages out to one tone and the outline reads as identical lumps; (3) NO PER-ROCK HUE IDENTITY - all rocks share the single warm VR_Rock tint with grayscale mottling, so the belt reads monochrome rather than a varied stone field. Scope: three rock passes (ST-283 COLOR_0 value + warm-hue re-bake on L/M/S, ST-284 geometry silhouette features on L/M/S, ST-285 in-code belt-read + special-rock audit) + ST-286 regression. BY LAYER - no core sim/scoring/replay-data changes; the collision radius = CONFIG.asteroid.sizes[size].radius (L 2.2 / M 1.3 / S 0.7) is FROZEN and the GLB extents must stay within +/-5% of it (assetGlbs.test.js invariant), so ST-284 geometry must break the silhouette with surface features that keep the bounding radius; no salt bump; demo pin structurally untouched (real-UI re-verify at ST-286). Grounded in the 3-shot I301 evidence + the CONTRAST principle-of-design study (Winged Canvas 7iwZEuT29vc - contrast = colour AND value + shape/form/line + texture, to create variety / visual interest / emphasis and lead the eye) + the value-first pipeline already logged in E21 (Thomas Brush 444K / Kyle Banks 229K) + E19 (Grant Abbitt bake-detail-into-low-poly). 4 stories; ST-284 (geometry) designated CUTTABLE.
